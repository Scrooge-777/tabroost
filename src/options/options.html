Workspaces

Edge-style Workspaces and persistent tab groups

General Behavior

Startup Window Recovery Automatically rebind restored browser windows to their workspaces after restart.
Close Window on Switch Close the current workspace window when switching to another workspace.
Confirm Workspace Deletion Show a confirmation prompt before deleting any workspace.
Auto-Sync New Tabs Automatically sync any newly opened or closed tab in the active window directly with the active workspace.
Drag Tab to Merge into Tab Group When moving or dragging one tab over another tab on the tab bar, automatically merge them into a tab group (Edge-style).

Backup & Restore

Export your workspaces and tabs to JSON for backups or transferring to another profile/device.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Open workspace switcher Ctrl+Shift+W
New workspace Ctrl+Shift+N

Customize shortcuts in brave://extensions/shortcuts

Danger Zone

Reset All Workspaces Permanently erase all saved workspaces, tab histories, and settings.
